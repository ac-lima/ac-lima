# Augusto C. Lima

**Geographer (BSc) · Earth Scientist (MSc) · Integrated Mountain Researcher**

*Exploring the interactions between the geosphere, cryosphere, and biosphere.*

I'm passionate about integrated mountain research. My academic and research journey, from geography and geomatics to glaciology and alpine ecology, has shaped my interdisciplinary approach to understanding mountain environments. I combine geospatial technologies, Earth sciences, and ecology to investigate how interactions between the geosphere, cryosphere, and biosphere drive landscape evolution and ecological change across spatial and temporal scales.
